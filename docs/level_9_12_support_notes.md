# Level 9-12 Support Notes

Levels 9-12 remain enabled. The current audit shows no blocking level-up choices for the supported band.

## Automated or Supported

- Spell progression, spellbook additions, Ritual Caster scaling, Weapon Mastery increases, Eldritch Invocation increases, Bard/Ranger Expertise, Sorcerer Metamagic, ASI/feat choices, and Warlock Mystic Arcanum are represented in the level-up flow.
- Contact Patron and Nature Speaker add their feature-granted spells to the finished sheet with casting metadata.
- Fiendish Resilience and The Third Eye are represented as editable rest-changeable configurations.
- Battle Master level 10 progression, Monk/Barbarian speed progression, subclass spell grants, subclass proficiencies, and subclass AC/resource values are covered by existing rules/tests where automated.

## Remaining Partial Support

- None. Circle of the Sea level 10 Stormborn is description-only by design because the WotC sheet has no dedicated fly speed or resistance fields; its feature text appears in Features and Traits.
- Active-play features such as Indomitable, Divine Intervention, Abjure Foes, Radiant Strikes, and similar combat timing features remain description-only by design.
